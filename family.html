Welcome to the Carter Family

Five people, two pets, and plenty of adventures.

Buddy is the family's friendly golden retriever, while Luna is a curious tabby cat who likes sleeping in sunny windows.

Our Last Family Vacation

The Carter family recently visited Washington, D.C. They toured the Smithsonian museums, walked around the National Mall, visited the National Zoo, and enjoyed an evening boat ride on the Potomac River. Sophia especially enjoyed the museums, while Mia loved the zoo.
